## What Works
- `/investimento` and `/servicos` are the *correct page types* for transactional/pricing intent — not buried in blog prose. Prices are bold, bulleted, scannable in the very first lines of each service card (evidence: `servicos.html` line 356 `<p style="color:#c8852a;font-weight:bold;">Preço: R$ 80</p>` pattern repeated for all 15 services).
- Chemical-differentiation answer ("Faz química? Não... Sem alisamento") is explicit, unambiguous, and marked up as FAQPage schema (faq.html lines 246-249) — directly serves the "avoids chemical straightening" persona with a citable, snippet-eligible answer.
- HairSalon/LocalBusiness schema on every page includes aggregateRating (4.9★/272), address, geo, and founder Person entity — strong trust signals for local-pack and AI Overview eligibility.
- `/metodo` (Método Leitura de Fio) is a genuine content-differentiation asset — no competitor found in the 4 SERP searches conducted had an equivalently named, systematized diagnostic method; this is a defensible unique-value page if promoted harder.
- FAQ page proactively answers volume/frizz/porosity objections a nervous first-timer would have (curly-hair-specific fear of "bad cut"), reducing pre-booking anxiety.
- Blog posts do link toward `/agendar` and `/investimento` at the end (evidence: curvatura-4c post line 434) — the connective tissue exists, it's just thin (see Finding 3).

## Findings

### Finding 1 — Pricing inconsistency on the transactional money-page (CRITICAL)
`/investimento`'s title tag says "Corte especializado... a partir de R$ 200" while its own noscript body two lines later says "R$ 190 a R$ 230," and the task brief states the flat price is R$190. Three different numbers for the same service across title/meta description/body is a trust and schema-validity risk (Google can penalize/ignore price rich-results with inconsistent on-page price signals) and directly undermines the "price-comparer" persona, who is explicitly primed by the local news SERP to distrust salon pricing.
**Recommendation:** Pick one canonical price (R$190 flat per brief) and align title tag, meta description, PriceSpecification schema (currently minPrice 190/maxPrice 230 — also inconsistent), and body copy across `/investimento`, `/servicos`, and homepage noscript.

### Finding 2 — /agendar (the conversion page) has near-zero crawlable content (HIGH)
The booking page's noscript fallback is 3 short paragraphs with no service list, no pricing recap, no trust signals (reviews, photos) — just an H1, a 3-step "how it works," and an address/WhatsApp line. Every other page on the site (home, servicos, investimento, sobre, faq) has a substantial noscript article; `/agendar` does not. Since this is the single conversion destination for every blog CTA and service page, it is the weakest-content page at the most important step of the funnel.
**Recommendation:** Add a richer noscript/SSR fallback to `/agendar` summarizing what's being booked, price recap, and 2-3 trust bullets (rating, "sem fila de espera," cancelamento flexível — per memory, cancel/reschedule already exists, so surface it here as a friction-reducer).

### Finding 3 — Thin, single, end-of-post CTA breaks the cold-blog-to-booking path (HIGH)
Evidence from the curvatura-4c post (495 lines): exactly one contextual CTA sentence appears, at the very last line of the article body, linking to `/agendar` and `/investimento`. There is no mid-article CTA, no sticky/repeated booking prompt, and no visual button-style CTA in the crawlable content (JS-rendered client view may add a floating button, but that can't be verified from this environment and shouldn't be the sole path for accessibility/SEO purposes). For a cold organic visitor landing 1,500+ words into a technical post, one text link at the bottom is high-friction — click-through depends entirely on reaching the very end.
**Recommendation:** Insert a mid-article CTA block (e.g., after the diagnostic/technical section) and standardize a repeated pattern across all 47 posts: 1 CTA after intro, 1 mid-article, 1 at close — each with distinct framing (soft/informational → direct booking) matching journey stage.

### Finding 4 — No content asset for comparison/"best-of" intent (MEDIUM)
"Melhor salão cabelo crespo BH" and similar comparison queries are won entirely by competitors and third-party listicles that don't mention Studio do Jon. No page on the site targets a "why choose us vs. other BH curl salons" framing, which is the format this SERP rewards.
**Recommendation:** Build one differentiation-focused page (or expand `/metodo` or `/sobre`) explicitly contrasting Método Leitura de Fio against generic salon approaches, written to be citation-worthy for future roundup articles, and pursue outreach/citations in existing BH "best salons" listicles.

### Finding 5 — Cluster fragmentation dilutes ranking authority on transição capilar and cronograma capilar (MEDIUM, cross-references cluster.md)
Both query intents split across 3-4 near-duplicate assets instead of one consolidated pillar (confirmed structurally in the prior cluster audit: "no visible pillar cross-linking hierarchy"). SERP evidence here confirms the cost: neither cluster surfaces in Google's actual top 10 for its head term, while single-asset competitors (institutes, brand guides) dominate.
**Recommendation:** Cross-reference `findings/cluster.md` Gaps 1-3; consolidate each cluster into one pillar page that absorbs or canonically out-links to spokes, matching the single-authoritative-asset pattern the SERPs reward.

### Finding 6 — Domain identity inconsistency in search-facing citations (LOW/INFO)
WebSearch for the branded query surfaced the business listed under `ojonquecortou.com` (no "www", no ".br") as a title/URL variant alongside the correct `www.ojonquecortou.com.br`. Could not verify from this environment whether this is a stale GBP/citation listing or an actual second domain; flagged for manual check against Google Business Profile and citation sources (NAP consistency), not confirmed as a live redirect issue.
**Recommendation:** Audit Google Business Profile primary website URL and major citations (Facebook, Instagram bio link, directories) for the exact canonical `https://www.ojonquecortou.com.br` to avoid split authority.

## Limitations
- Could not render the live JS-hydrated DOM in this environment (no Playwright/Python available); all page-content analysis relies on the site's own prerendered `<noscript>` fallback, which the repo's own convention (per memory: prerender.js) treats as the SEO source of truth, but this means floating/sticky CTAs, modals, or client-side-only trust elements (e.g., a booking-widget preview) were not observable and could partially offset Finding 2/3.
- SERP analysis used WebSearch (Google-backed but not a literal SERP scrape) — actual ranking positions, featured snippets, and PAA boxes were not directly captured; findings are based on which domains/content types dominate result sets, which is directionally reliable for page-type/intent-match conclusions but not a substitute for a live rank tracker.
- Did not audit `/glossario`, `/galeria`, or individual service subpages beyond `/servicos` and `/servicos/transicao-capilar` in full depth.
- No Google Search Console or Analytics data was consulted in this pass (a prior GSC audit exists per memory — cross-reference for actual click/impression data on these queries rather than inferred SERP presence).
